## Hero — Jensen Huang returns as Nvidia CEO

  director on Monday 23 November, 11 days after he resigned; shares rose

## Denmark's top court backs 'ghetto law' evictions
- Denmark's Supreme Court ruled on Monday 23 November that ending 91
  leases in the Nøjsomhed estate in Helsingør under the 2018 'ghetto law'
  was not discrimination on ethnic grounds, backing the country's hard
  line on immigration. The EU's top court had ruled last year the law
  could amount to ethnic discrimination.
- Sources: Reuters (Copenhagen, 23 Nov 2026), SRN News, Al Jazeera.

## 34 workers still missing after Indonesian candy factory fire
- A fire swept through the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia candy factory (Milkita)
  in Cikande, Banten, on Saturday 21 November; as of Monday 23 November
  rescuers were still searching for 34 missing workers, with remains in
  24–26 body bags awaiting forensic identification. About 300 workers
  were inside; 29 were rescued alive.
- Sources: Associated Press (23 Nov 2026), Reuters (22 Nov 2026).
